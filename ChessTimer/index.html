Chess clock

Round Table

One clock for the whole table. Everyone gets the same bank of time; only the player on turn burns it.

Players

4 seats

Time per player

10:00minutes each

Seats are numbered from the top. The brass hand points at whoever is on the clock, and play passes clockwise with a tap anywhere.

ROUND TABLE
Tap to start

Tap anywhere to pass  ·  Space to pass  ·  P to pause
